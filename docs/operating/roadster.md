# Stationary Roadster display

The entrance plaza contains a silver angular Roadster-inspired car above the replaceable Atlas sponsor panel. It rests, ignites white/cyan jets, rises, hovers, jumps through a full roll, descends and lands over one fixed pad. The display uses the existing native Tiled animated-tile pipeline; it adds no visitor script, bot behavior, travel, interaction, collision or WAM metadata.

The 160×160 frame sits at tile(73,60), spanning five tiles each way. Its visible pixels fit the conservative arrival camera band; transparent right padding extends beyond that band for the left spawn column. The chosen space is visibly blue reception terrace, with no overlap with props, sponsor panels, entries, exits, WAM areas or any of the 792 audited guide cells. An older underlying terrain rectangle describes the main avenue, but its visible terrain is covered by the reception terrace here. The root visual review accepted this placement.

`art/source/roadster-poses.png` is the original generated image (SHA-256 `08b8e46661d57bb4f44ea0e09bdee91debc3311440d1d2406a949e164bd86cd0`). It is actually 1254×1254, despite the generation prompt requesting 1024×1024. The full prompt and authoring parameters are retained in `roadster-authoring.json`. Private photographs were visual references only and are not distributed. No sponsor logo is baked into the display. Cold-gas jets are stylized animation, not a technical simulation of a real vehicle.

## v1 pose compile

The compiler divides the source at 0,313,627,940,1254, finds each pose's alpha bounds, applies one 0.4 nearest-neighbor scale, thresholds alpha to 0/255 and centers it at integer coordinates over the fixed pad. `art/source/roadster.json` records each source crop, target rectangle, timing and exact inputs. The v1 cycle is sixteen whole-pose frames: landed 3s, ignition 0.6s, rise 0.5s, low hover 0.7s, hover 1.2s, jump 0.5s, six roll poses 0.35s each, recovery 0.5s, descend 0.8s, touchdown 0.6s and rest 2.3s.

## v2 compiled motion

`art/source/roadster-v2-authoring.json` steps the same sixteen poses, in order, at 100 ms over the same 12.8 s cycle (landed 3.0s, ignition 0.6s, rise 0.5s, low hover 0.7s, hover 1.3s, jump 0.5s, six roll poses 0.3s each, recovery 0.6s, descend 0.9s, touchdown 0.6s, rest 2.3s). Every car pixel still comes from the v1 crop recipe; the compiler synthesises 128 steps, merges consecutive identical steps (87 frames) and packs them with the same 32px carrier scheme. Per-frame receipts (`frames[]` in the metadata: phase, pose, vertical offset, blend source, glow intensity and placed glow pixels, shadow level and half-width, pad light state, dust frame, light levels) make every effect testable.

- **Smooth motion.** Each phase declares its motion: `ground` (fixed), `tween` (the drawing enters at the previous phase's final roof height and eases in/out to its own placement, optionally `endDy` px lower), `roll` (a 100 ms dithered ghost of the previous and current drawings, then the pose) or `drop` (ease-in fall to the pad, then a 2 px / 1 px settle). Low hover and hover add a ±2 px sinusoidal bob (1 s period) once their tween has settled; the bob offset carries into the next tween so nothing snaps. The first and last frames are the grounded v1 poses at their exact v1 anchor.
- **Thrusters.** Plumes and puffs are located in each pose by colour class (pale cyan seeds grown two pixels into their white cores; blobs of at least 12 pixels, 6 seeds and 6 rows). Beneath each one the compiler adds a cyan/white bloom: ordered-dithered 255-alpha pixels over the transparent terrace, additive light on the pad and on the plume pixels themselves. Intensity cycles through three variants (0.85/1.0/1.15) every 100 ms while airborne, ramps up at ignition, and is brightest (1.3×) at ignition, jump and touchdown; the six short roll poses hold one steady variant after their ghost frame to stay inside the tile budget.
- **Ground effects.** A soft elliptical shadow darkens the pad under the car; it narrows from a 54 px to a 30 px half-width and fades over four altitude levels. While airborne the pad's centre bar carries two chasing cyan segments (200 ms stride) and the end strips alternate; ignition blinks the strips, touchdown flashes bar and strips, grounded holds leave the pad static. Ignition and touchdown each emit a three-frame expanding ring of pale dust/ion dots at pad level.
- **Lights.** The thin white front strip (bright pixels in the lower-left of an upright body) pulses softly through three levels on a 1.6 s period. The red rear bar flares at ignition and touchdown (hot core plus a two-pixel additive halo for 300 ms, then a one-pixel halo) and brightens through the descent.
- **Roll.** Every roll transition (jump→roll-1 … roll-6→recover) starts with a 100 ms 50 % checkerboard ghost of both drawings so the full roll reads as continuous motion blur.

Binary alpha is preserved everywhere: effects on transparent terrace are dithered 255-alpha pixels, effects on the pad or the car are colour changes only, and every visible pixel stays inside x < 144 and off the frame edges.

Compile both epochs with `node tools/run-tool.mjs tools/art/roadster.ts` (it refuses to run if the frozen v1 atlas no longer reproduces). The compiler writes only atlases and metadata. When deliberately revising the animation, refresh the descriptors through `node tools/run-tool.mjs tools/maps/author-airborne-birds.ts` (which copies the exact v2 `overlay` into the overworld layout, migrating a v1 descriptor if it finds one, and reconciles the native through the projection gate), then `npm run maps:build`, `npm run art:check`, the Roadster/native-context/guide tests, `npm run buildmap` and the source checker. Preserve existing layer IDs/GIDs and fields: the roadster layer keeps id 57 and its 25 cells; only its GIDs move to the v2 interval. Do not refreeze the original 303 inventory or alter unrelated revision entries.

The guide map guard includes decorative geometry. Each epoch therefore requires a re-audited geometry receipt and a newly built module pin; guide cells, routes, controller behavior and guard enforcement are unchanged. The module/config pins must move together. Existing deployed browsers are not evidence that a new map/module pair works live. Actual live animation and the new guide startup remain coordinator acceptance gates; local evidence uses Tiled 1.12.2 and the checksum-pinned deployed Phaser parser for every frame and the loop boundary.
